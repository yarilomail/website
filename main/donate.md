# Donate

YariloMail is an independent open-source mail server, licensed under AGPL v3
and funded by the people and companies who use it. There is no venture money
behind it and no paid tier holding features back.

Donations pay for:

- the servers the test stand runs on, where every release is measured before it
  ships: load tests, failovers and a rollback to the previous version;
- the hosts the FreeBSD and Linux packages are built and checked on;
- the tools and the time it takes to keep up with security fixes, protocol RFCs
  and long-term maintenance.

## Ko-fi

One-time or monthly donations by card or PayPal, at
[ko-fi.com/yarilomail](https://ko-fi.com/yarilomail). A monthly membership
gives the project income it can plan around.

## thanks.dev

For companies that fund the open-source software they depend on:
[thanks.dev/u/gh/yarilomail](https://thanks.dev/u/gh/yarilomail). thanks.dev
splits a budget across the dependencies it finds in your repositories, so
YariloMail gets its share when you run it.
